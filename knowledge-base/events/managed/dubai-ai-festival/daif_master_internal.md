# Dubai AI Festival internal master reference file

## Purpose
This file is an internal reference document for the Dubai AI Festival (DAIF) series. It is intended for future knowledge-base ingestion and AI retrieval workflows using only verified information contained in the uploaded post-event reports for this series batch.

## Internal-use status
Internal-only working reference. This file is not intended for public distribution, external publication, or promotional reuse without separate review.

## Editorial rules used
- Source base limited to the uploaded reports: `dawf-24-post-event-report-2.pdf` and `daif-25-post-event-report.pdf`.
- No outside sources were used.
- No missing information was inferred.
- The canonical series name used in this master file is **Dubai AI Festival**.
- The first edition’s original branding, **Dubai AI & Web3 Festival**, is preserved only within the relevant edition entry.
- Where a report includes wider ecosystem, AI Week, or umbrella-level programming, only event-specific content has been extracted where separable.
- Where figures appear to describe broader festival or umbrella activity and cannot be cleanly separated, they are identified as combined-event or umbrella-level metrics.
- OCR-sensitive or inconsistent items are retained cautiously and flagged in the relevant data-quality notes.
- Chronology is preserved based only on the two uploaded reports in this batch.

## Coverage in this file
This file currently covers two editions in chronological order:
- Dubai AI Festival 2024 Dubai (original report branding: Dubai AI & Web3 Festival)
- Dubai AI Festival 2025 Dubai

This file does not establish any broader series history beyond what these two uploaded reports confirm.

## Interpretation cautions
- The 2024 report presents the event as **Dubai AI & Web3 Festival**, while the 2025 report uses **Dubai AI Festival**. For master-file consistency, both are treated as part of one canonical series: Dubai AI Festival.
- The 2024 edition includes AI and Web3 together, while the 2025 edition appears AI-only in branding, themes, and positioning.
- Both reports include multiple sub-events, partner-led activations, roundtables, showcases, and ecosystem programming. These should not automatically be interpreted as equivalent standalone editions within the series.
- The 2025 edition is explicitly presented as part of **Dubai AI Week**, so some listed activities and positioning may sit within a wider umbrella environment even when featured in the report.
- Some metrics in both reports refer to the broader festival footprint rather than only plenary-stage conference attendance.

## Critical cautions
- Missing editions in this file must not be treated as proof that no other editions took place.
- The 2024 and 2025 reports use different naming, structure, and emphasis, so direct year-on-year comparisons should be made carefully.
- Partner, exhibitor, sponsor, and media rosters are report-specific and should not be assumed to carry across editions unless explicitly confirmed.
- Testimonials, quotes, and statements from officials and speakers are preserved as reported and should be treated as attributed remarks rather than independently validated claims.
- Metrics tied to AI Week, ecosystem events, MoU signings, media value, digital impact, or associated programmes should be treated as report-specific indicators, not normalized series KPIs.

### Overview / summary
Blockchain Business Conference 2017 is described as a two-day event held in Vizag, positioned within Andhra Pradesh’s FinTech Valley narrative. The report states that the conference brought together regional and international blockchain experts, startups, corporates, FSI executives, regulators, entrepreneurs, policy makers, and academics to explore blockchain technologies, applications, and expected impact on financial services.

The report also frames the event as part of a broader vision to transform Vizag into a global IT and fintech ecosystem. Across its content, the conference is presented not only as an event platform but also as a convening point for government use cases, investment discussions, startup challenges, and ecosystem-building announcements.

### Quantitative metrics
Headline event metrics presented near the front of the report:
- 1200+ attendees
- 60+ speakers
- 40+ startup exhibitors
- 20+ countries
- 10+ industry associations

Associated activity metrics and report-stated numerical references:
- Around 40+ CEOs from local and international companies participated in the precursor golf tournament.
- A one-day student workshop took place on 8 October 2017 at GITAM University, Visakhapatnam.
- The report’s highlight section states: 9 companies are on board, 15 companies will start operating shortly, and 35 companies have given assurance of investment.

Media-coverage and report-embedded numerical claims linked to the surrounding ecosystem, retained as reported rather than harmonized with the headline totals:
- One article says representatives from 15 countries and about 100 speakers were attending.
- One article states about 2,500 delegates, including 500 from abroad, were attending the conference.
- Media coverage cites 18-odd companies agreeing to set up operations in the city.
- A report-reproduced article says the Cashless Vizag initiative reached about 72% adoption and about 10,000 merchants switched to cashless transactions.
- Media pages also cite broader state targets such as 1 lakh IT jobs and 2 lakh electronics jobs by 2019, as well as investment goals and square-footage targets for IT infrastructure; these are ecosystem claims associated with the event coverage, not event attendance metrics.

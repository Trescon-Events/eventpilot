## Bengaluru Skill Summit 2025

Source file(s): bss-25-post-event-report.pdf

### Original event name used in report
Bengaluru Skill Summit

### Event metadata
- Series: Bengaluru Skill Summit
- Edition: Inaugural (1st edition)
- Edition year: 2025
- City/location: Bengaluru, Karnataka, India
- Organiser: Department of Skill Development, Entrepreneurship & Livelihood, Government of Karnataka
- Event Curation & Management Agency: Trescon (appointed by KSDC — Karnataka Skill Development Corporation, a Government of Karnataka undertaking)
- Patronage / inauguration: Inaugurated by Hon. Chief Minister Sri Siddaramaiah and Hon. Deputy Chief Minister Sri D.K. Shivakumar
- Division: Managed Events
- Report type: Standalone post-event report

### Overview / summary
The inaugural Bengaluru Skill Summit brought together leaders across government, industry, education, and skilling to advance Karnataka's skill development agenda and unveil the state's landmark Skill Development Policy 2025–2032. The event served as the flagship platform for the Department of Skill Development, Entrepreneurship & Livelihood and positioned Bengaluru as India's skilling capital.

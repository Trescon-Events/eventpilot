# World CX Summit internal master reference file

## Purpose
This file consolidates factual information from available World CX Summit post-event reports for internal knowledge retrieval, internal analysis, and future AI-assisted query workflows. It is not intended for external publication.

## Internal-use status
Internal working master file compiled from attached World CX Summit event reports available in this batch.

## Editorial rules used
- Only facts visible in the source reports are retained.
- Missing facts are not inferred.
- Weak OCR sections are either omitted or flagged in data-quality notes.
- Virtual-event engagement, registration, and social metrics are preserved as report-specific figures and are not standardized across editions.
- Where sponsor labels or headings are visible, they are preserved as stated in the reports.
- Missing editions in this file must not be treated as proof that the event did not happen.

- World CX Summit 2020 Jakarta Virtual
- World CX Summit 2021 Asia Virtual
- World CX Summit 2022 ASEAN Virtual
- World CX Summit 2022 India / Mumbai

## Interpretation cautions
- Series naming varies across editions, including Jakarta, Asia, ASEAN, India, Mumbai, Bengaluru, and Singapore descriptors.
- The file now combines virtual and in-person editions, so engagement, registration, attendance, and networking metrics are not directly comparable across all years.
- Some speaker names, sponsor names, and industry counts are affected by OCR noise in the extracted text and should be treated carefully where noted.
- The 2023 Singapore edition was part of a larger co-located Singapore event environment, so WCS-specific metrics are retained separately from broader event-complex references where visible.

## Critical cautions
- The 2021 Asia report contains weak OCR in the sponsor section, so sponsor names are not captured at full-confidence from that file.
- The 2021 Asia attendee-breakdown industry counts appear OCR-compressed in places and are retained only at high confidence.
- The 2023 Singapore report includes references to co-located activity and DATE launch context; only World CX Summit-specific facts are treated as edition-level content.
- The 2024 Mumbai report includes several media-outreach figures and some pre-event publicity statements on coverage pages; these are retained as campaign/report figures and not treated as standardized series totals.
- The 2024 Bengaluru report contains some OCR drift in speaker-company alignment and partner-tier formatting; only high-confidence entries are retained.

# HODL Series internal master reference file

## Purpose
This file is an internal master reference for the HODL series, which comprises the legacy World Blockchain Summit (WBS) series and the post-rebrand HODL Summit edition. It is intended to serve as structured source material for internal knowledge retrieval, reporting support, speaker and sponsor intelligence, event positioning, and historical series analysis.

## Internal-use status
Internal-only working document. It is not written for external publication, marketing distribution, or public citation. It preserves report-level claims as stated in source documents and is designed for future AI retrieval and internal reference.

## Editorial rules used
- Only attached source reports were used.
- Facts were extracted conservatively; low-confidence OCR material was either omitted or flagged.
- Promotional, audience, media, and campaign statistics were retained as report-specific claims and not normalized across years.
- When exact sponsor, partner, exhibitor, or media-partner names were not readable from OCR, the category was retained without forcing name-level reconstruction.
- Chronology has been preserved.
- The 2025 HODL Dubai edition is treated as the continuation and rebrand of the WBS line, not a separate unrelated series.
- Missing editions in this file must not be treated as evidence that an edition did not take place.

## Interpretation cautions
- Report design and reporting style vary sharply by year; early editions are often narrative and sponsor-logo heavy, while later editions are metric-driven decks.
- Some 2018-2024 decks contain image-led sponsor and partner sections where category labels are legible but logo text is not recoverable from OCR.
- Website, media, social, and database statistics appear to be campaign-report figures for a specific edition and should not be treated as directly comparable standardized KPIs across all years.
- Some reports mix event recap with future-event promotion and broader portfolio positioning; only event-relevant information has been prioritized.
- The 2025 HODL report includes series-level legacy claims that refer to the broader lineage of the summit brand after rebranding.

## Critical cautions
- This file is not a complete census of all editions in the series.
- Apparent differences in attendee totals, speaker counts, investor counts, and sponsor counts may reflect reporting design rather than methodology consistency.
- Weak OCR in some files may underrepresent exact person, sponsor, and partner names.
- Where exact name-level detail could not be verified, category-level reporting has been preserved instead of inferred completion.

# Dubai Silicon Oasis (DSO-DTEC) — FutureTech Ecosystem Proposal

## Overview
A proactive Trescon proposal to DSO-DTEC (Dubai Silicon Oasis and its startup engine DTEC) to build a three-platform FutureTech innovation and ecosystem engagement strategy from 2026 to 2027. Three source files cover the same proposal at different levels of detail: a one-page teaser, a full executive deck, and a credential-led version.

## The Proposed Event Concept

**Name:** FutureTech Ecosystem (Three-Platform Model)

Trescon proposed not one event but a sequenced three-platform stack designed to move ideas from research to capital to global market entry — with DSO-DTEC positioned as the regional anchor for frontier technology.

### Platform 1 — STRIDE (September 2026)
- Full name: Science, Technology Research, Innovation & Development
- Role: Research Engine
- Scale: 500 curated participants
- Purpose: Convert applied R&D into pilots, PoCs, and commercial deployment powered by DTEC
- Audience: Universities, R&D leaders, corporate innovation teams, government stakeholders

### Platform 2 — Future VC (November 2026)
- Full name: Future Venture Capital Forum
- Role: Capital Engine
- Scale: 300 curated delegates, 100+ investors, 35 startups
- Purpose: Convert startup pipeline into funding, partnerships, and DSO-DTEC establishment
- Audience: Founders, VCs, family offices, corporate VCs

### Platform 3 — Dubai FutureTech Festival (March 2027)
- Role: Global Flagship
- Scale: 5,000+ delegates, 300+ investors, 100+ speakers, 100+ exhibitors, 60+ countries
- Purpose: Position DSO-DTEC as the regional anchor for frontier technology and future-economy sectors
- Audience: Governments, investors, corporates, academia, startups globally

## Seven Priority Technology Clusters
PropTech, AI, Robotics, Mobility, MedTech, Cybersecurity, Deep/Quantum

## Strategic Rationale
DSO is uniquely positioned — DTEC as startup engine, Dubai Digital Park as pilot environment, District IO as R&D expansion — to own the "conversion layer" the region is missing. Most events stop at visibility. DSO-DTEC can deliver pilots, capital, and company establishment. No single event dedicated to FutureTech in Dubai. No prominent tech event in Q1 Dubai calendar.

Alignment: Dubai D33 Agenda, UAE Digital Economy Strategy, Operation 300bn, Vision 2030.

## Competitive Landscape Addressed
GITEX (large-scale, not conversion-led), LEAP (general tech, not DSO-owned), STEP (limited investor conversion), DeepFest (narrow thematic scope), AI Everything, Dubai AI Festival, Dubai AI Week, Web Summit Qatar.

## Commercial Model
- DSO-DTEC covers 100% of direct event costs including venue, production, hospitality, speaker travel, and Trescon's management and operations fee
- DSO/DTEC responsibilities: focal point for government coordination, chair of Steering Committee, ministerial participation, ecosystem sponsorship facilitation
- Trescon responsibilities: conceptualisation, planning, management, partnership/sponsorship strategy, logistics, production, content programming, stakeholder engagement

**Indicative Total Investment (Year 1):**
- STRIDE: AED 1M
- Future VC: AED 1M
- Dubai FutureTech Festival: AED 7M
- **Total: AED 9M**

## Revenue Drivers
Sponsorship, exhibition sales, country partners/pavilions, government support, eventual ticket sales.

**Investment Recovery Trajectory:**
- Year 1: 20–40% (establishment and proof of concept)
- Year 2: 40–70% (expanded sponsorship)
- Year 3: 70–100% (financial sustainability)

# Uzcosmos — World Space Economy Summit, Central Asia Edition

## Overview
A proactive Trescon proposal to Uzcosmos (Uzbekistan's national space agency), curated and managed by Trescon, to launch the World Space Economy Summit — Central Asia Edition in Tashkent in 2027. Under the patronage of the Ministry of Digital Technologies of Uzbekistan.

This is Trescon's entry into the space economy sector and Central Asia as a market — the most geographically novel proposal in the BD vault.

## The Proposed Event Concept

**Name:** World Space Economy Summit — Central Asia Edition
**Tagline:** Positioning Uzbekistan as the strategic catalyst for practical space solutions — from orbit to impact.
**Mandate:** From Orbit to Impact
**Organised by:** Uzcosmos
**Curated & managed by:** Trescon
**Under the patronage of:** Ministry of Digital Technologies, Uzbekistan
**Location:** Tashkent, Uzbekistan
**Year:** 2027
**Format:** 2-day high-level forum (Day 1: Science & Vision; Day 2: Capital & Outcomes)

**Target scale:**
- 300–500 delegates
- 30–50 speakers
- 40+ investors
- 30+ exhibitors

## The Market Gap

**Central Asia's space inflection point:**
1. Rapid digitisation across nine economies; Uzbekistan–2030 targets 100% high-speed coverage
2. Satellite demand skyrocketing (~14,000 active satellites in orbit, early 2026)
3. Vast territories with transboundary resources (agriculture, mining, logistics) modernising in parallel
4. Region moving from consumer to co-creator of space technology

**The missing layer:**
No unified platform. No high-level forum that converges sovereign policy, strategic capital, and orbital technology into one structured dialogue in Central Asia.

## Why Uzbekistan

**National mandate:** National Space Program 2035 — three vectors: human capital, sovereign space & ground segments, and "smart specialisation" in lunar and Mars exploration.

**Sovereign capability:**
- First national satellite by 2028
- Selection of first national astronaut in progress
- Domestic Earth-observation constellation in development
- Partnerships formalised with SpaceX, ispace, Airbus, Maxar

**Regional authority:**
- Chair, CIS Space Council
- Future host of IAC 2028 in Samarkand "Space City"
- Unified regulatory bridge across the Eurasian market

**Uzcosmos live operations:** 12M hectares monitored, 100K+ regional insights generated, 11 government sectors integrated

## Four Strategic Objectives
1. Unite governments, investors, and space leaders in one high-level convergence point
2. Accelerate real-world space applications (precision agriculture, water management, LEO connectivity, border security)
3. Position Uzbekistan as the regional hub (cement CIS Space Council mandate + IAC 2028 hosting)
4. Attract global capital and OEMs — channel FDI and sovereign funds into the National Space Program 2035

## Six Content Pillars
1. SatCom & Connectivity — Silk Road 2.0
2. Earth Observation — Sovereign EO
3. AI & Space Data — Downstream IQ
4. Aerospace & MRO — Industrial Hub
5. Policy & Security — Spectrum & Orbit
6. Investment & PPP — Capital Architecture

## Programme Logic
**Day 1 — Science & Vision:**
Ministerial keynotes, policy sessions on spectrum/orbital access, CEO-led strategic panels, live tech showcases, applied use-case deep dives

**Day 2 — Capital & Outcomes:**
Closed-door investor roundtables, curated B2G and B2B deal rooms, MoU signings on stage, sovereign-to-OEM matchmaking, pilot project commitments

Narrative arc: Policy → Pilots → Capital → Outcomes

## Audience Segments
- A·01 Government & Defence (Ministers, Regulators)
- A·02 Investors (Sovereign, VC, PE)
- A·03 Industry & OEMs (Operators, Manufacturers)
- A·04 Startups (Geospatial, AI, SatCom)
- A·05 Academia (Research, Astronomy)
- A·06 Media (Tier-1, Regional)

## Event Enablers
**Pre-event:** Policy dialogues with Uzcosmos, sponsor onboarding/briefings, delegate curation/vetting, press activation

**During:** Capital Rooms (closed-door deal flow), Policy Studio (sovereign roundtables), Tech Showcases (live demos), VIP networking and ministerial dinners

**Post-event:** MoU tracking and reporting, investment activation, pilot project kick-offs, year-round working groups

## Expected Outcomes

**Tangible / Measurable:**
- MoUs signed (Uzspace ↔ global aerospace OEMs)
- Investment secured (ground segments, MRO, Space City)
- Pilot projects launched (transboundary water, LEO connectivity, border EO)

**Strategic / Long-term:**
- Regional leadership (CIS Space Council, IAC 2028 host)
- Policy alignment (unified Central Asian space market)
- Global positioning (architect of digital sovereignty)

## Commercial / Partnership Model
**Trescon delivers:** Conceptualisation, planning, management, partnership/sponsorship/marketing strategy, logistics, production, content programming, stakeholder engagement, revenue generation

**Uzcosmos provides:** Direct event costs (5-star hotel, production, hospitality, speaker/VIP travel, Trescon management/operations fee), ministerial participation (President or Prime Minister level), high-level keynote addresses, inter-ministerial roundtables, ecosystem sponsorship facilitation via Uzcosmos letters, focal point for coordination with ministries

# SPARK — Sharjah Next: Sustainability Technical Proposal

## Overview
A joint technical proposal by Trescon and Wizcraft Entertainment LLC submitted to Sharjah Research, Technology & Innovation Park (SPARK) for the role of Lead Event Production & Management Partner for "Sharjah Next: Sustainability" — a two-day global sustainability innovation forum.

**Note:** This is a joint bid — Trescon partnered with Wizcraft Entertainment LLC. Any future reference to this proposal should acknowledge the joint nature of the submission. Access is restricted to management level due to the third-party partner involvement and detailed production cost implications.

## The Proposed Event

**Name:** Sharjah Next: Sustainability
**Subtitle/Theme:** Frameworks of the Future: Shaping Tomorrow
**Concept:** Innovation for a Sustainable Future
**Venue:** Sharjah Research, Technology & Innovation Park (SPARK), Sharjah, UAE
**Date:** 28–29 October 2026
**Scale:** 700 participants (delegates, media, and partners)
**Format:** 2-day global convergence forum

## Event Objectives

1. **Global Positioning** — Position SPARK as a leading global hub for sustainability innovation and technology transfer
2. **Innovation & Technology** — Showcase breakthrough solutions across AgriTech, Water Innovation, Environmental Solutions, and Clean Energy
3. **Strategic Collaboration** — Connect governments, innovators, startups, investors, and industry leaders
4. **Investment & Commercialisation** — Drive investment opportunities and strategic partnerships
5. **Future Sustainability Platform** — Establish Sharjah Next: Sustainability as a flagship annual international platform

## Design Concept
Trescon and Wizcraft proposed an event design language called "Frameworks of the Future" — inspired by modular architectural frameworks, open structures, and sustainable materials. Key design principles:
- Simplicity with purpose
- Sustainable elegance
- Innovation through design
- Human-centered experiences
- Modern minimalism and natural materiality

The event environment was designed to function as a "living ecosystem of sustainability" — architecture, materials, storytelling, technology, and interaction working together.

## Event Sectors Addressed
AgriTech, Water Innovation, Environmental Solutions, Clean Energy

## Trescon Credentials Referenced
Dubai FinTech Summit, Dubai AI Festival, HODL/WBS series, Future Sustainability Forum (particularly relevant — same sector), DATE

## Notes for Future Reference
- This proposal is primarily a design/production proposal, not a strategic/concept proposal like most others in the BD vault. It demonstrates Trescon's ability to respond to production-heavy RFPs.
- The "Frameworks of the Future" concept name and sustainability design language is a strong reusable creative concept for any sustainability/ESG event pitch.
- The joint bid with Wizcraft is notable — Wizcraft brought design/production expertise (IIFA Awards, Nike ADT Expo, Burj Azizi Launch, World Padel League). This partnership model (Trescon as event management lead + specialist production partner) may be worth repeating for large design-intensive events.

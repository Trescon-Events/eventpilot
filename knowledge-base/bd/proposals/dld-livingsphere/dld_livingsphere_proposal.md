# Dubai Land Department — LivingSphere Summit Technical Proposal

## Overview
Trescon's technical proposal response to RFQ-623-2025 issued by Dubai Land Department (DLD). Proposes management of the LivingSphere Summit — a global platform hosted by DLD focused on future-ready sustainable, smart, and human-centred urban development. Dates TBD for 2026.

## The Proposed Event Concept

**Name:** LivingSphere Summit
**Tagline:** Shaping Tomorrow's Communities / "Where land, life, and technology converge"
**Host:** Dubai Land Department (DLD)
**Format:** Public summit with exclusive, vetted attendance
**Target scale:** ~2,000 visitors
**City:** Dubai, UAE

**Positioning:** A niche, curated summit (vs Cityscape's 20,000+, RISE Expo's 15,000+, IPS Dubai's 16,000+) — DLD as host and driver, with direct policy enactment and ecosystem integration.

## Market Context

**DLD's market position:**
- $207B+ transaction value in 2024 with 110,000+ new investors
- 9.9% rise in prime property values forecasted
- Regulatory pioneer: blockchain registry, smart contracts, Ejari overhaul
- Strategic driver behind Real Estate Strategy 2033 and Urban Master Plan 2040
- Global catalyst for sustainable urban development

## Eight Key Objectives for DLD
1. Policy Leadership — position DLD as a global benchmark in land governance
2. Digital Transformation Acceleration — advance blockchain registry, smart contracts
3. Investment Attraction — promote Dubai as investor-ready real estate hub
4. Future-Ready Urban Design — encourage resilient, livable city design
5. Cross-Sector Collaboration — unite government, private sector, academia
6. Sustainable Development Advocacy — ESG, green finance, inclusive urban innovation
7. Public Engagement & Legacy Building — inspire civic pride
8. Trust & Transparency — include public voices in land/housing dialogues

## Seven Content Themes and Pillars
1. Urban Leadership & Land Governance
2. Hyper-Connected Urban Intelligence
3. Climate-Positive Growth & Resilient Infrastructure
4. Iconic Design & Human-Centric Urbanism
5. Digital Transformation & Smart Permitting
6. Investment Attraction & Economic Leadership
7. Capacity Building & Knowledge Ecosystems

## Event Format and Stages
- Plenary Leadership Stage — national and global leaders on regulatory innovation and investment strategy
- Investor Track — real estate finance, fund insights, ESG assets
- Knowledge Capsule Theatre — quick talks, firesides, policy showcases
- Closed-Door Roundtables — stakeholder challenges, regulatory alignment
- Design Gallery — competition finalist showcase
- Real Estate Intelligence Zone — smart city data, dashboards, regulatory insights
- Media Studio — live sessions, interviews, content creation
- Digital Innovation Track — PropTech, AI, blockchain, smart permitting

## Unique Activations
- Smart Community Walkthrough — immersive neighbourhood demo (mobility pods, zoning kiosks, green facades)
- PropTech Startup Zone — curated exhibition of emerging PropTech startups
- Innovation & Policy Labs — hands-on sessions tackling Ejari reform and title deed digitization
- Urban Design Competition & Gallery — entries from children, students, and design firms
- LivingSphere Dialogues (pre-summit) — invite-only closed-door roundtables
- "Design Your Dubai" Public Campaign — residents share visions of future communities
- "Living Landscape of Tomorrow" Video Series — micro-documentary interviews distributed pre-event

## Target Audience
Government leaders and urban regulators, Architects and master planners, C-suite from real estate / PropTech / infrastructure, ESG experts / legal advisors / compliance officials, Global institutional and private investors, Media / think tanks / academics

## Competitive Positioning vs Dubai Real Estate Events
| Event | Focus | Scale | LivingSphere Differentiator |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cityscape Global | Mega projects, global sales | 20,000+ | LivingSphere: policy + tech + ESG, not just sales |
| RISE Expo | Investment, smart cities | 15,000+ | LivingSphere: DLD-hosted, niche and curated |
| IPS Dubai | Real estate transactions | 16,000+ | LivingSphere: direct policy enactment |
| PropTech Connect | Real estate technology | 6,000+ | LivingSphere: broader policy + investment mandate |

## Partnership with Government Entities
Joint initiatives with Dubai Municipality, MOCCAE, DEWA, and others for urban policy alignment.

# Oman Investment Authority — STRIPE Oman Proposal

## Overview
A proactive Trescon proposal to the Oman Investment Authority (OIA) to launch STRIPE Oman — the Sultanate's premier transport, logistics, and infrastructure investment forum. Designed to catalyse FDI, foster innovation, and facilitate strategic collaboration between government, industry, and investors aligned with Oman Vision 2040 and the National Logistics Strategy 2040.

## The Proposed Event Concept

**Name:** STRIPE Oman
**Full form:** Supply Chain • Transportation • Roads • Railways • Infrastructure • Ports • Expo
**Tagline:** Building Smart Transport, Logistics & Supply Chain Ecosystems for Vision 2040
**Hosted by:** Oman Investment Authority (OIA)
**In partnership with:** Trescon (organised by)
**Format:** 2-day flagship investment forum
**Launch year:** 2026

**Year 1 target scale:**
- 3,000+ on-site attendees
- 100+ exhibitors
- 100+ speakers and thought leaders
- 25+ countries represented
- 45% international participation
- 10 PPP/investment MoUs announced
- 1,000+ B2B/investor meetings facilitated
- 100+ hosted investors/delegations
- 40+ global media partners
- Envisioned investment pipeline: USD 10M – 50M

## Strategic Rationale

**Oman's positioning:**
- Unique geographic crossroads connecting Arabian Gulf, Indian Ocean, and East Africa
- World-class deep-water ports: Duqm, Sohar, Salalah
- Oman Vision 2040 and National Logistics Strategy 2040 target logistics as 2nd largest GDP contributor by 2040
- Transport and logistics currently 7% of GDP; national target 10%+ by 2040
- OMR 2.5 billion investment (2021–2025) in port expansions, airport upgrades, multimodal corridors

**The gap:**
No single high-level, investment-focused platform that converges government, investors, developers, operators, and innovators to accelerate project execution and attract global capital into Oman's transport and logistics sector.

**National agenda alignment:** Oman Vision 2040, National Logistics Strategy 2040 (MTCIT)

## Main Theme
**Integrated Mobility & Logistics for Sustainable Growth**

## Six Sub-Themes / Tracks
1. Supply Chain Resilience & Future-Ready Logistics
2. Road Infrastructure & Freight Efficiency
3. Rail Connectivity & Cross-Border Trade
4. Digitalisation, AI & Smart Mobility
5. Ports, Maritime & Free Zones Development
6. Policy, Regulation & Workforce Development

## Four Core Strategic Pillars
1. Mobilise Investment at Scale — present bankable project pipeline, attract global institutional investors
2. Enable Public-Private Partnership Execution — structured PPP matchmaking, translate policy into commercially viable projects
3. Align Policy with Market Readiness — high-level dialogue between regulators and industry leaders
4. Champion Innovation & Sustainable Infrastructure — smart mobility, digital customs, automation, green corridors
5. Elevate Oman's Regional Leadership — position Oman as gateway connecting Asia, Africa, and Europe

## Target Audience — Key Sectors
Government & Public Sector, Transport Infrastructure & Operations, Logistics & Supply Chain, Financial Services & Investment

**Target regions:** GCC, MENA, South Asia, East Africa, Asia-Pacific, Europe

## STRIPE Oman Differentiators vs Regional Events
| What Peers Do Well | Gap | STRIPE Oman Feature |
|---|---|---|
| UAE: global players & zone marketing at scale | Fewer Oman-specific bankable pipelines | Deal Room + Project Pipeline Book with data rooms and term-sheet templates |
| Saudi: events tied to sovereign cheques | Investors need policy clarity + PPP visibility | PPP Investor Forum with MTCIT/OPAZ/OIA briefings on risk allocation |
| Singapore: tech + green narrative | No Green Logistics focal conference on Gulf-Indian Ocean lanes | Green & Digital Corridors Track + MoUs for pilots at Oman's ports |
| UAE/Saudi: limited site tours | Investors want to see plots and infrastructure before committing | On-site SEZ/port tours (Sohar, Duqm, Salalah) with fast-track investor desks |
| All three: strong brands | Oman needs a signature annual investment moment | STRIPE as Oman's annual FDI marketplace under MTCIT patronage with recurring KPIs |

## Commercial / Partnership Model
- **IP:** STRIPE Oman co-owned by Oman Investment Authority & Trescon
- **OIA provides:** Direct event costs (5-star hotel, production, hospitality, speaker/VIP travel, Trescon management/operations fee), ministerial participation, ecosystem sponsorship facilitation, coordination with ministries and federal authorities
- **Trescon delivers:** Conceptualisation, planning, management, partnership/sponsorship/marketing strategy, logistics, production, content programming, stakeholder engagement, revenue generation
- **Revenue:** Sponsorships, exhibitions, possibly ticketing
- **Revenue sharing:** Net revenue post-expense reconciliation split 50:50 between Trescon and OIA
- **Governance:** Joint Steering Committee with OIA, Trescon, and key stakeholders; monthly progress review meetings

## Notes for Future Reference
- The "STRIPE" acronym model (Supply Chain • Transportation • Roads • Infrastructure • Ports • Expo) is a strong naming technique — highly memorable and sector-descriptive. Consider similar acronym-based naming for other infrastructure/logistics proposals
- The Project Pipeline Book concept (pre-vetted, bankable project list released at the event with data rooms and term-sheet templates) is a powerful innovation that makes the event a genuine investment marketplace — reusable for any FDI-focused event pitch
- The on-site port/SEZ tour concept is distinctive and differentiates from typical conference experiences
- This proposal represents Trescon's first entry into the transport/logistics/infrastructure sector — useful reference for any similar pitch in GCC or South Asia
